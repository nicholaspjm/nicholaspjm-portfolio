about

Nicholas Marriott is a creative technologist based in Naarm / Melbourne, b. 1999 in Aotearoa New Zealand. He works in real-time systems as a visual designer, performer, and exhibiting artist, across a variety of mediums.

His visuals have featured on major stages and screens, including The xx's 2026 festival tour and a Grammy-winning music video for Nine Inch Nails, alongside commercial work for Nike and live performances at festivals including Pitch Music & Arts. His installation work extends these systems into physical space, most recently a large-scale reactive light environment for Chadstone. His practice has been profiled in It's Nice That and Rolling Stone.

I’m available for commissions, art direction, teaching, and speaking.

Abstracted Organica

2026

Series term coined by Gabriella Brown for It's Nice That

A recent series spanning multiple practices, where Australian landscapes meet real-time biomimicry (DLA, Physarum). A term coined by Gabriella Brown for It's Nice That. Featured artist.

“Nicholas shifts between the geometric and the organic, at times leaning into the grid-like, techier realm of TouchDesigner, and at others into hypnotising formations that reflect patterns in wind or cellular growth. He creates generative visualisers that feel alive, like roots spreading or spores dispersing, mimicking processes seen in nature. While it's easy in TouchDesigner to get lost in a sea of neon (trust me, I've been there), Nicholas grounds his pieces with earthy tones of ashy blues, browns, black and grey. These palettes make his visualisers immersive without being overwhelming. They enhance spaces rather than dominate them, resulting in genuinely beautiful works of art.”

Gabriella Brown, It's Nice That

“Abstracted Organica”, a term coined by Gabriella Brown, was a perfect way to encapsulate a recent series of work that spanned across multiple practices in early 2026. The culmination of inspiration from Australian landscapes and real-time biomimicry-style algorithms (DLA, Physarum, and others) resulted in this.
